Clarivate named me to its 2026 list of Highly Cited Researchers on 18 November. I first appeared on the list in 2023, and this is my fourth consecutive year.

This year’s list includes 6,802 researchers from 62 countries and regions. To compile it, Clarivate looks for researchers with several papers that rank in the top 1% by citations among papers published in the same field and year. Its analysts then review the wider influence of that work. [Clarivate’s 2026 announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/clarivate-reveals-highly-cited-researchers-2026-list-302942057.html)

Much of my work with students and colleagues has been about making evolutionary algorithms practical on larger problems. One project that grew out of that work is [EvoX](https://www.evox.group/), where we share tools for running those algorithms in parallel on GPUs. My thanks to everyone who has worked on the papers, written code or tried the algorithms on a problem of their own.
