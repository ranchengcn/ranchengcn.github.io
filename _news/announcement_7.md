I’m honored to have been named a **Highly Cited Researcher for 2026** by Clarivate. This is my fourth consecutive year on the list, following my inclusion in 2023, 2024, and 2025.

Clarivate released the 2026 list on November 18. It recognizes **6,802 researchers** from more than **1,200 institutions** across **62 countries and regions**. Those researchers received **7,090 designations** in total, as some qualified in more than one field. Of these, 3,839 were awarded across 21 Essential Science Indicators (ESI) fields, and 3,251 were awarded in the Cross-Field category. Based on researchers’ primary affiliations, Hong Kong accounted for 147 designations, ranking ninth globally on that measure. [Clarivate’s 2026 announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/clarivate-reveals-highly-cited-researchers-2026-list-302942057.html)

In its 2025 analysis, Clarivate reported 6,868 researchers across 60 countries and regions. [Clarivate’s 2025 analysis](https://clarivate.com/highly-cited-researchers/analysis/)

Clarivate’s Institute for Scientific Information compiles the list annually to recognize researchers whose work has had a broad influence in their fields. For the 2026 edition, it examined publications indexed in the Web of Science Core Collection from **2015 to 2025**. Selection starts with researchers who have authored multiple papers in the **top 1% by citations** among papers in the same field and publication year. The Cross-Field category captures research influence that extends across disciplines. [Research and citation criteria](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/news/nine-manchester-researchers-named-among-worlds-most-influential/)

Clarivate also applies additional quantitative checks and expert review to assess the breadth of that influence. Its research integrity checks look for patterns such as excessive self-citation and unusual publication or citation activity, taking into account typical practices in each field. [Clarivate’s 2026 evaluation and selection overview](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/clarivate-reveals-highly-cited-researchers-2026-list-302942057.html)

I’m grateful to the students and colleagues who have worked with me on evolutionary algorithms over the years, and to the [EvoX](https://www.evox.group/) contributors who have shared code, tested algorithms, and helped others use our work.
