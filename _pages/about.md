I am currently an Associate Professor with the [Department of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/people/academic-staff/cheng-ran/) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). I received the BSc degree from Northeastern University (China) in 2010 and the PhD degree from the University of Surrey (UK) in 2016. Between 2010 and 2012, I spent a cherished period at Zhejiang University as a postgraduate student.

My research philosophy centers on _benefiting humanity by bridging nature and technology_. I develop evolutionary AI-native systems for the complex physical world, bringing together evolutionary computation, generative AI, and modern computing infrastructure. My research explores how designs, models, and software can continually evolve through task feedback and rigorous validation, and how validated advances can be retained, reused, and built upon to support scientific discovery and engineering innovation. As an important foundation for this research, I initiated the <a href="https://evox.group/" style="white-space: nowrap;"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/favicon-192x192.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" aria-hidden="true" style="width: 1.05em; height: 1.05em; margin-right: 0.16em; vertical-align: -0.16em;">EvoX</a> open-source project, which enables efficient and scalable evolutionary computation on modern heterogeneous computing infrastructure through tensorization and parallel execution.

I am the founding chair of [IEEE Computational Intelligence Society (CIS) Shenzhen Chapter](https://r10.ieee.org/shenzhen-cis/). I am serving as an Associate Editor/Editorial Board Member for several journals, including: _ACM Transactions on Evolutionary Learning and Optimization_, _IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation_, _IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computational Intelligence_, _IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems_, _IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence_, etc.

I am the recipient of the _IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation Outstanding Paper Awards_ (2018, 2021), the _IEEE CIS Outstanding PhD Dissertation Award_ (2019), the _IEEE Computational Intelligence Magazine Outstanding Paper Award_ (2020), and the _IEEE CIS Outstanding Early Career Award_ (2025). I have been featured among the _World's Top 2% Scientists_ and the _Clarivate Highly Cited Researchers_. I am a Senior Member of IEEE.
